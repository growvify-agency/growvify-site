Questions

Good to know before we start

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many reels you need each month, shoot days and whether you want ads. After a free growth call you get a clear written quote, with no hidden charges.

How long before we see results?

We work on a 90-day roadmap. The first 30 days are for setting up and testing, days 31 to 60 double down on what works and start ads, and days 61 to 90 scale it. You get a review with real numbers every 30 days.

Will my reels go viral?

Some might, but nobody can promise it. We build a system that doesn't depend on luck: tested content, consistent posting and ads behind the winners.

Do we need to run ads from day one?

Usually not. We start organic to find what works for free, then add ads from the second month. Local brands that need walk-ins or orders quickly can start a small ad test earlier.

Do you guarantee sales?

No one honestly can. What we promise is planned content, consistent posting and properly run ads, with clear numbers every month so you can see what's working.

Who pays for the ads?

You pay the ad spend directly to Meta from your own account. Our fee covers setting up, running and improving the campaigns.

Do you come to our location to shoot?

Yes. Plans include up to 2 shoot days a month at your brand's location in Hyderabad.

What if I don't like an edit?

Every reel includes 2 revision rounds, so you approve it before it goes live.

Can you only edit videos we shoot ourselves?

Yes. We also take edit-only work for reels and ad creatives. Message us with what you have.
